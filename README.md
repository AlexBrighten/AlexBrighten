### 🧐 The Confession: A PM on GitHub

Welcome to the corner of GitHub where nobody is flexing a 500-day LeetCode streak.

I live at the intersection of **UX Design**, **Operations**, and **Engineering**, but my brain fundamentally operates as a **Product Owner**. I am sitting on a BCA degree (Class of 2026) and accidentally learned full-stack architecture along the way. 

Why did I learn how to engineer full-stack systems if I’m not a developer?

> **"I learned to code just so developers can't lie to me about how long a feature takes to build."**

I respect the code, but I care about the user journey, the business unit economics, and the go-to-market strategy. I am far more excited about reducing onboarding churn by 30% and asking engineers *"Why?"* five times before they commit to an over-engineered sprint backlog.

---

### 🎯 What I Actually Do

- **Ask "Why?" Five Times:** Before anyone writes a schema or spins up a microservice, I make sure we are solving a real customer problem—not building solutions in search of problems.
- **Kill Feature Creep Early:** Shipping 3 high-impact features beats shipping 15 bloated ones that require an onboarding manual.
- **Translate Chaos into Roadmaps:** Turning angry customer Zendesk tickets and vague stakeholder wishes into prioritized user stories, PRDs, and Figma specs.
- **Optimize Funnels (Digital & Real-World):** Funnels aren't just web forms. A slow vendor onboarding flow or a physical queue of 3,000 delegates is just friction waiting to be engineered out.
